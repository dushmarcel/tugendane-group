Tugendane Group • Tugendane Rides

Privacy Policy & Personal Data Protection

Effective Date: October 2026 • Aligned with the principles of Rwandan Law N° 058/2021
Carpooling Regulatory Model: Tugendane Rides is a community cost-sharing marketplace for pre-planned shared trips in private vehicles across Rwanda. We do not provide commercial taxi-hailing services.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tugendane Group ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you use the Tugendane Rides application and web platform (Package ID: com.tugendanegroup.rides).

2. Information We Collect

To provide community carpooling and ensure trip security, we collect:

  • Account Credentials: Full Name, phone number (MTN / Airtel Rwanda), and email address.
  • Identification Details: Rwandan National Identification Number (NIN) collected for passenger and driver trust. Format (16 digits) is validated upon entry; full verification is subject to administrative review.
  • Vehicle & Driver Details (Drivers Only): Vehicle make/model, color, registration plate number, and driving license details.
  • Trip & Booking Data: Departure point, destination corridor (e.g. Kigali, Huye, Musanze, Rubavu), departure time, seat requests, and trip notes.
  • Emergency SOS Data: Live trip details transmitted only upon explicit user request to emergency contacts or Rwanda National Police (112).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data strictly for:

  • Facilitating direct communication between confirmed drivers and passengers.
  • Verifying user identity and preventing fraudulent listings.
  • Calculating transparent fuel cost-sharing contributions (including the standard 5% platform fee).
  • Dispatching emergency assistance when the SOS function is triggered.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We respect your privacy: we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to commercial advertisers.

Your phone number and pickup location are only shared with the confirmed driver or passenger for that specific scheduled trip.

5. Payment Processing

Fuel cost contributions are transacted directly via authorized Rwandan telecommunication channels (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or cash at pickup). We do not store financial payment PINs or private bank details.

6. Data Security & Storage

User credentials and trip records are encrypted and stored via Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure in compliance with industry standards and Law N° 058/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy in Rwanda.

7. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. To request deletion, email privacy@tugendane.rw or use the profile settings within the app.
